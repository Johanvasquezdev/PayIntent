# PayIntent — 44-second ad and walkthrough

The programmed film in `presentation/video` carries all necessary on-screen copy. If narration is added in an editor, use this timed script and keep the music underneath it.

| Time | Voiceover |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | A payment request should not be a guess. Is it personal, or are you buying something? |
| 0:04–0:10 | PayIntent understands the purpose before you choose how to pay. |
| 0:10–0:18 | Describe the payment in everyday language. AI interprets the intent and extracts the important details. |
| 0:18–0:26 | When the purpose is unclear, PayIntent asks one focused question instead of taking action. |
| 0:26–0:35 | For a business invoice, you review the exact recipient, amount, currency, and item. Your approval creates an unsent PayPal Sandbox draft only. |
| 0:35–0:41 | The recipient is not notified, and no money moves. The model proposes. Fixed rules control the action, and every decision is traceable. |
| 0:41–0:44 | PayIntent. Understand the payment. Choose the right path. Stay in control. |

## Capture plan

1. Start with the ambiguous request `Send $50 to Carlos` and pause on the focused clarification question.
2. Select `I'm buying an item` to show that PayIntent explains the commercial path rather than sending a personal transfer.
3. Enter the invoice example, then pause on the exact draft preview and its `NOT SENT` badge.
4. After explicit approval, capture the success state: `Invoice draft saved`, `Recipient not notified`, and the local activity record.

Only record the Sandbox flow. Keep credentials, account email addresses, and any real transaction data out of the capture.

## YouTube checklist

- Export at 1280×720, 60 fps, H.264/AAC.
- Keep the final public video below three minutes.
- Do not show API keys, real customer data, or production transactions.
- Use only the included original score or music for which you hold permission.
- Confirm the YouTube video is public before adding it to Devpost.
